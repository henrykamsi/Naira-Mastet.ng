Privacy Policy for Naira Master

Last updated: September 2026

At Naira Master, operated by Henry Global Tech Industry (accessible via www.henryglobaltech.name.ng), safeguarding the privacy of our visitors and registered users is a top priority. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Naira Master and how we utilize it.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an account on Naira Master, we collect basic identification credentials such as your name, surname, email address, and encrypted password authentication details to secure your account wallet and user session.

2. Task & Wallet Data Tracking

We process data concerning user task submissions, categorizing them into Approved Tasks, Declined Tasks (with timestamps), and Pending Tasks, alongside internal wallet actions ('Add Money' and 'Withdraw Money') to maintain transparent financial records for each member.

3. Google AdSense and Cookies

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on Naira Master. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our platform and/or other sites across the internet.

4. Contact Information

If you have questions or require more information regarding our Privacy Policy, please reach out via our official website or support channels.
